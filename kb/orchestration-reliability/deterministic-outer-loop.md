# Keep the outer workflow deterministic and adaptive reasoning bounded

## What

Keep the outer workflow deterministic and adaptive reasoning bounded

Agents may iterate locally, but only explicit policy gates should move the workflow between stages; local intelligence need not imply arbitrary global transitions.

Test the smallest supported intervention on representative tasks. Compare against a baseline, check correctness and failure cases, and retain a rollback before wider use.

## Expected effect

- **Cost:** Fewer full restarts and no-progress loops
- **Speed:** Less polling, contention and recovery delay
- **Quality:** Checkable intermediate and final outcomes
- **Reliability:** Bounded recoverable state transitions
- **Certainty:** Expected directions, not measured local gains; savings across clusters overlap and must not be added.
